## Behaviour

A toggle reacts when the button goes down, not when it comes up, as on hardware and in desktop DAWs. `behavior` sets what a press does:

| `behavior` | Press | Release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"toggle"` | Flips the state. | Nothing. |
| `"momentary"` | Turns it on. | Turns it off. |
| `"hybrid"` | Flips the state. | Flips it back if it was held longer than `holdDelay` (250 ms), like the buttons of controllers such as Push. |

<kbd>Space</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd> do the same from the keyboard. Inside a [toggle group](/docs/components/toggle-group), toggles can also be painted by dragging.

`onPressedChange` receives the reason for each change: `"press"`, `"release"`, `"keyboard"`, `"paint"` or `"exclusive"`.
